# Identity checklist (before finishing)

## Signature items: does it look like Abhinav's system?

Count how many are clearly present in the screenshots. Low needs 3 of 7, Medium 5 of 7, High 7 of 7 (`intensity.md`).

1. **Night and Paper:** a Night shell or page with Paper where things are read or written.
2. **Washi fibres:** faint lines visible at 100% on Night (light) and Paper (dark); coloured blocks carry grain.
3. **Serif voice:** Cormorant for titles, names and display lines; sans for everything long.
4. **Kicker pair:** an uppercase gold kicker over a serif heading, at least once.
5. **Colour jobs:** gold = current/progress, lacquer = selected/done/mentioned, sakura = unread/new. Nothing else coloured.
6. **The ink pill:** one primary button, the ink pill that turns white on hover.
7. **A portfolio motif doing work:** the moon (empty state, ring), the book spread, the scene on a first-run or hero, petals of sakura for "new".
